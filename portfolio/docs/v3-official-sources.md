## CIM Level 4

[Qualification page](https://www.cim.co.uk/qualifications/certificate-in-professional-digital-marketing/), [qualification planner](https://www.cim.co.uk/qualifications/certificate-in-professional-digital-marketing/level-4-qualification-planner/), [full syllabus PDF](https://www.cim.co.uk/media/ktpns1sn/cim-l4-certificate-2024-quals-spec.pdf)

The official title is **CIM Level 4 Certificate in Professional & Digital Marketing**. The Certificate has four modules totalling 50 credits. The planner specifies:

1. Marketing Impact is mandatory, worth 20 credits.
2. Choose either one core plus two specialist modules, or both cores plus one specialist module.
3. Each core and specialist module is worth 10 credits. An additional module leads to the Extended Certificate, a separate 60-credit qualification.

The current qualification page uses the labels **Mandatory**, **Core** and **Specialist**. Its available modules, with concise paraphrased descriptions, are:

| Module | Official group | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing Impact | Mandatory | Marketing foundations, customer behaviour, research and tactical planning. |
| Responsible Marketing | Core | Trust, inclusion, sustainability and ethical brand decisions. |
| Planning Integrated Campaigns | Core | Coordinating channels and measuring campaigns across the customer lifecycle. |
| Content Marketing | Specialist | Planning formats and content around audience journeys and organizational goals. |
| Search Engine Optimisation | Specialist | Search visibility, SEO success factors and paid search campaign foundations. |
| MarTech | Specialist | Using technology, AI and analytics to support customer relationships. |
| Social Media Marketing | Specialist | Channel selection, content planning and evaluating social activity. |

This is a curriculum overview, not a claim that the owner selected or completed all seven available modules. Use **CIM Level 4 student** or **In progress**, as supplied by the user.
