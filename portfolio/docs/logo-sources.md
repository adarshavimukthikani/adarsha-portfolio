The role is **Public Relations Coordinator, Circle of Marketing**, supplied by the owner. The university page provides organizational context, not appointment verification. Its small source logo is rendered at a modest size to retain quality. The University of Ruhuna crest is the university emblem; its source is an official recruitment subdomain, not a claim that the owner studies at a different faculty.

Original logo colours and aspect ratios are preserved. White or dark artboards provide readable surfaces in both themes. The Moratuwa asset has original white lettering, so it uses a dark artboard. Logos identify education, learning, and tools without implying endorsement. Rights remain with the respective owners.

The campus photograph supports the actual education story and is credited on that page. The official website does not supply an open reuse licence; it is not represented as royalty-free. No campus or organization imagery was generated.

Original downloads are in `docs/source/logos/`. `node scripts/prepare-brand-assets.mjs` reproduces public files from them. The saved Photoshop original comes from the user's existing project; all other logos were retrieved from official servers.
